Privacy Policy

TapTile · Last updated November 27, 2026

TapTile is a tile-matching puzzle game. You can play without creating a game account. Progress is saved on your device. The Android app includes advertising, analytics and crash-reporting services that may process device and usage information.

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to the TapTile Android app and this information website. For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer using the support email on the app's official Google Play listing. The Android application identifier is com.match3games.puzzle.

2. Game data on your device

The game stores settings, language, level progress, stars, coins and earned rewards in local app storage. It does not require a personal profile or a player account, and this version does not provide multiplayer rooms or a developer-operated cloud save service. Android or your device provider may separately offer backup or restore features according to your device settings.

3. TapTile gameplay analytics

TapTile sends gameplay events to a developer-operated analytics service. We use these events to measure how players progress from launch to a completed level, identify where sessions or attempts stop progressing, and troubleshoot gameplay and ad reward flows.

  • App use: launching the game, entering the foreground, moving to the background, and opening the home screen.
  • Gameplay: starting a level, selecting an available tile, a tile arriving in the tray, clearing a set of three, winning, failing, continuing after failure, and leaving or restarting an unfinished attempt when the app can observe the transition.
  • Settings and ads: changing the game language, requesting a rewarded or level-completion interstitial ad through the game, and receiving a rewarded-ad result (reward earned, reward not earned, or request timed out).

Each report includes the event type, time, sequence within the session, level number where applicable, app version, game language, platform, and a test-build flag. Randomly generated identifiers distinguish the installation, session, level attempt, and event. These identifiers let us group gameplay steps and avoid counting repeated uploads. The gameplay event records exclude names, email addresses, GPS coordinates, and advertising IDs. Cloudflare receives the connection IP address and the ingestion service uses it for rate limiting. The event table does not store IP addresses.

The app queues events locally and sends them in batches over HTTPS, including after connectivity returns. Its upload window is seven days. Server-side gameplay records are subject to a 90-day retention policy, with older records removed by scheduled cleanup.

5. Permissions and network access

The app uses internet and network-state access for gameplay analytics, ads, service initialization, diagnostics and Google Play update functionality. The gameplay does not request access to your contacts, photos, messages, microphone, camera or precise location. It does not provide device-cleaning or device-management functionality. Ads may open an advertiser's website or a store page, where that service's policy applies.

6. Use and disclosure

Local progress supports gameplay and rewards. Gameplay events support progression analysis and troubleshooting. Technical information sent through the integrated SDKs supports advertising, measurement, app operation and troubleshooting. We do not sell local game saves or request your name or email to play. Advertising providers may use information across their services as described in their policies; this page does not claim that all SDK data stays on your device or that the app is tracking-free.

7. Your choices and deletion

You can reset local progress by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes the local analytics queue. Previously uploaded data remains subject to the applicable retention policy. You can manage your advertising ID and available ad-personalization controls in Android or Google settings. Options depend on your Android version and region. Use the provider links above for their privacy controls, or contact the developer through Google Play for access, correction or deletion requests. We may need enough information to identify the data involved.

8. Retention, security and international processing

Local saves remain in app storage until removed or reset, subject to device backup settings. Providers determine retention for the technical data they process under their service policies and configuration. Processing may take place outside your country. Android app-storage controls and provider security measures help protect information, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

9. Children

TapTile does not ask players to enter their age or personal details to play. Parents or guardians with questions about a child's information should contact the developer through the app's Google Play listing and may also use the relevant provider's privacy request process.

10. This website

These pages are static and contain no account form, advertising SDK, analytics script or first-party tracking cookie. The hosting provider may process basic request information such as IP address and browser details to serve and secure the website. External policy links take you to third-party websites.

11. Updates

We will update this page when the app's data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.
